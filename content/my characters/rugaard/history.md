Rugaard suffered a head injury as a small child, leaving him with long-term amnesia and partial paralysis (this is best described as left hemiparesis and full left paralysis below the knee). His left side in general is less responsive than the right; and below the knee his left leg can't move at all. When out and about he prefers to use a crutch (or two) as it helps his self-esteem and sense of independence. He has a wheelchair for at-home and longer stays away.

He experience's other symptoms as a result of his brain injury which you can find detailed on his "medical" write up page here on the site.

After his injury, he was removed from his biological father's care and spent about a year in recovery (moving from hospital to rehabilitation facility and eventually to out-patient). He possesses no knowledge of the events which occurred before his fall and instead, based his personality and sense of self on his DnD notebook which was one of the only things on his person brought with him to hospital. 

Most of his teenage years were spent in care facilities and boarding school as a result of his father's parenthood being revoked due to gross neglect. He is a ward of the country in which he resides. Too old (and undesirable) to be fostered out, Rugaard spent his time as ward until he was an adult, where he transitioned into adulthood like any other adult. 

 If his teachers had to describe Rudy in a word, it'd usually be "_something_" - his tendency to get into trouble started young due to a strong feeling of self-righteousness. Even if it means taking the L himself, Rudy will go out of his way to ensure what he sees as 'fair' is what gets done."

As a parentless child, Rugaard qualified for various later-education aids and went to college and dormed for the duration of his education. He acquired a Bachelor's in Journalism as well as the various credentials he needed to perform his job legally and proficiently. He also has various certifications in some miscellaneous technological studies to aid in the case of which journalism doesn't pan out in the way he desires. 

He currently resides in Berneray, Scotland, UK in a small one floor cottage residence with his cousin, Alis. She is paid generously by his father to help him around his home and take care of him.
