"Rugaard is a young man who can easily come off as rather egotistical, though kind. He often holds a front of over-confidence in his abilities as a cover for feelings of self-doubt (_not that he'd ever admit it_). He enjoys attention and showing off to feel important. His primary interests lie in fantasy and media analysis.

Not unlike his cousin, who aims to become a horror novelist, Rudy himself enjoys both consuming and making stories. Given a deep-rooted streak of petty delinquency, the ability to spin a yarn on the fly has gotten him out of many a sticky situation. He has a habit of exaggerating his motions to compensate for his left side, and speech patterns that are verbose and whimsical on a good day. Some of his friends find his mannerisms charming or even funny - others may need a strong coffee before opting to deal with him.

Rugaard suffered a head injury as a small child, leaving him with long-term amnesia and partial paralysis. His left side in general is less responsive than the right; and below the knee his left leg can't move at all. When out and about he prefers to use a crutch as it helps his self-esteem and sense of independence. He has a wheelchair for at-home and longer stays away.

Most of his teenage years were spent in boarding school since his late father was often absent. If his teachers had to describe Rudy in a word, it'd usually be "_something_" - his tendency to get into trouble started young due to a strong feeling of self-righteousness. Even if it means taking the L himself, Rudy will go out of his way to ensure what he sees as 'fair' is what gets done." - Limb's Original
