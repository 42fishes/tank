Seen below are Rugaard's things that he keeps on his person. You can find them sorted by container and then items within their respective containers.

**Rugaard's Bag** - the bag varies from whatever he is up to on any given day. sometimes it is a sling backpack, sometimes a messenger bag, sometimes a roller bag if he is travelling.

**Rugaard's Laptop** - rugaard uses a macbook air 13in because it is easy to carry, thin and has a nice screen for the price. he appreciates the long battery life. 

**Rugaard's 1st Cellphone (Personal)** - rugaards main phone is just one provided by his carrier. he doesn't particularly care about what sort of phone he uses. 

**Rugaard's Crutches** - rugaard uses a pair of green forearm crutches 

**Rugaard's Binder** - his binder is his offline organizational tool for things he doesn't feel secure putting on the web. everything from appointments to his ledger is in his binder.

**Rugaard's Tech Pouchette** - 
a usb c to micro sd and sd card converter - used with his laptop to read, write and flash sd cards
a usb c to a dongle - to be used with a devices on c ports on his laptop 
a 3.5mm to bluetooth converter - converts normal headphones to be used via devices without a headphone jack
a 4-1 charging cable - one side is c and a the other is micro, lightening and c in
a 65w slim charging brick - small and easy to store, it is thin and narrow 
a micro pry bar - a small discreet pry bar
the second cellphone  - the second cellphone is a burner, it runs postmarketOS
